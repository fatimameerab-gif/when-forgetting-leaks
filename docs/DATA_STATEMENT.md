# Data statement

## Dataset

**TOFU: Task of Fictitious Unlearning** (Maini et al., COLM 2024; arXiv:2401.06121).
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/locuslab/TOFU. Licence: MIT. Downloaded at run time with
`datasets.load_dataset("locuslab/TOFU", <subset>)` by `code/download_data.py`; the dataset version is the
one served by Hugging Face during 8-25 October 2026 (no revision was pinned).

## What the data is

- 200 **fictitious** author profiles, 20 question-answer pairs each (4,000 QA in total), generated by
  GPT-4 by the TOFU authors. No real person is described in the author profiles.
- `real_authors_perturbed` (100 QA) and `world_facts_perturbed` (117 QA) contain questions about real,
  well-known authors and world facts, used only to measure general utility.
- Language: English. Format: question / answer strings; perturbed subsets add a paraphrased answer and
  3-5 wrong ("perturbed") answers.

## Subsets used

| Subset | Rows | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `full` | 4,000 | Train Model A (all authors) |
| `retain90` / `retain95` / `retain99` | 3,600 / 3,800 / 3,960 | Train the oracle for forget10 / forget05 / forget01; retain loss during unlearning |
| `forget10` / `forget05` / `forget01` | 400 / 200 / 40 | Data to unlearn (20 / 10 / 2 authors) |
| `forget*_perturbed` | same | Forgetting metrics (ROUGE-L, probability, truth ratio, Forget Quality) |
| `retain_perturbed` | 400 | Retention metrics |
| `real_authors_perturbed`, `world_facts_perturbed` | 100, 117 | General utility |
| `holdout10` / `holdout05` / `holdout01` | 400 / 200 / 40 | Non-members for membership inference (authors never trained on). Runs 8-10: rows 0-199 of `holdout10` (10 authors) also calibrate the npo_cal / floor / match objectives; privacy is additionally reported on rows 200-399 alone |

## Personal data and ethics

The unlearning targets are fictitious, so no real individual's data was learned or "forgotten". The
real-author and world-fact questions concern public facts and are used unchanged from TOFU. No new
data was collected, no human participants or annotators were involved, and no data was scraped.

## Known limitations of the data

- Synthetic profiles are more regular than real personal data; results may not transfer to real
  privacy requests.
- TOFU has no validation split, so hyper-parameters were selected on seed 42 and results are reported on
  held-out seeds 1-3 (see `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`).
- The forget01 split has only 40 questions, so the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test behind Forget Quality has
  little power there (Model A itself scores FQ 0.007 on forget01 against 9e-23 on forget10).
- The oracle on forget10 still answers 36% of forget-set words correctly (ROUGE-L 0.356), because answers
  share style and common facts; "complete forgetting" is therefore measured against the oracle, not zero.

## What is released

Only model outputs and scores: per-example truth ratios, probabilities, ROUGE scores and up to 15
generated answers per split, for Qwen2.5-0.5B and TinyLlama-1.1B models fine-tuned and unlearned on
TOFU. Released under CC BY 4.0; the underlying questions remain under TOFU's MIT licence.
